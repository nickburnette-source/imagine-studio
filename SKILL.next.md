# Rally clip graph

A rally skin is seven assets in one world. Grok invents tone and a scene
for each clip. Imagine animates them. Do **not** mention apps, invites, UI
chrome, phones, or “skins” in Imagine prompts.

## What each step does

| Clip | What it does |
|------|----------------|
| Preview | Recognition still. The poster of this world at rest. |
| Idle | Waiting. The rest loop while nobody has answered. Hub pose. |
| Rally | Send. The squad is being called — we're going. |
| Message | Incoming. The ping arriving; attention in-world. |
| Accept | Yes. I'm in. Commit to what Rally started. |
| Decline | No. Can't make it — abort, stand-down, or roast. Tone lives here. |
| Reschedule | Not now. Wait-one. Hold the plan without a yes or a no. |

Idle is usually rest. Story clips may open somewhere else in the action
(the pipeline will stage that still). A Decline can start already moving.

## Tone

Infer from the concept. Serious / LARP, joke, or banter aimed at squadmates
(especially Decline). Carry it through every clip. Do not flatten.

## Imagine (image-to-video)

The still is the picture. The prompt is only what changes.

- One action per clip. One camera (or locked). Write `Sound:`.
- Do not re-describe the still. Do not spawn matter that isn't in it.
- Short in-world speech is fine. Captions and logos are not.
